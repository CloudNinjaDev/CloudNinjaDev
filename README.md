### 🧠 What I actually work on

- **Multi-cloud & hybrid infra** — designing and running workloads across AWS, GCP, and Azure, plus bare-metal clusters when the cloud bill stops making sense
- **Kubernetes, the hard way** — self-managed k8s on bare metal, Helm charts, GitOps rollouts, and the 2am etcd debugging that comes with it
- **Networking** — VPNs, P2P/mesh connectivity, and tying multi-cloud + on-prem networks together securely
- **Resilience** — Velero-driven backup/restore and disaster recovery that's actually been tested, not just configured
- **Observability** — building metrics/logs/tracing stacks that surface real signal, not dashboard noise
- **Cost & process** — auditing infra spend and dev workflows to cut waste without slowing teams down
- **Security-first delivery** — pushing IaC, CI/CD, and coding practices that bake security in instead of bolting it on
